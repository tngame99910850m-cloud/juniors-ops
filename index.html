The day we met

I was just thinking about the day we met—you in that green outfit with your broken hand. You had this incredibly beautiful face, and eyes so striking they stopped me cold. I remember joking around with you, and you playfully punishing me with your small hand. Then your mom actually telling you to give me your Instagram! That was such a beautiful day. I really felt like I had met an angel. XD

November.2024

Our first real conversation

And then when we started chatting on Instagram, I didn't even have internet at my house, so I went all the way to a coffee shop just so I could message you. We ended up talking for over 12 hours straight. We realized we had the exact same mentality, and honestly, that was the day I felt like I needed you in my life.

December.2024

A place that's ours

Then there was that day we sat in the cafe. I had my espresso, you had your cappuccino, and the second we held hands, I felt like I was on cloud nine. I remember thinking right then and there how much I loved you.

October.2024

The moment I knew

Do you remember when I kissed you? You told me you thought I was just trying to touch you or that I was playing around. I wanted you to know that wasn't true at all. I wasn't just trying to take something from you or treat it like a game. I kissed you simply because I loved you so much.

Julie.2025

A hard day we got through

Then came the day we broke up, which is the day I hate looking back on the most because I know how much I hurt you. I am so deeply sorry for everything that happened that day.
